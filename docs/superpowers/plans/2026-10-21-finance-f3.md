## 文件与接口边界

领域：compliance/models.py、storage/f3_*.py、storage/store.py和common/business_allocations/f2_checks的最小投影接点及领域测试。网络：compliance/provider.py/protocol.py/queue.py/evidence.py。归档：compliance/archive*.py及专用测试。root：API/runtime/config、业务包/人工页、联调文档。实现子代理顺序派发，独立读审可并行。

领域方法configure_compliance(policies,providers,*,simulation=False)、verify_invoice(who,id,expected_version,key)、get_invoice_compliance、list_invoice_verifications、get_compliance_options、list_compliance_plans、list_tax_confirmations、list/get/refresh_red_letter_case、link_red_letter_invoice、list_tax_adjustments、record_tax_adjustment_evidence、list/get_archive、get_archive_manifest/tests/evidence、list_archive_supplements、audit_archive/get_archive_audits。列表沿用F2分页模式；具体签名以类型定义为准。公共preview/getplan/execute/reviewQueue/getoperation/cancel/reconcile按f3plan_/f3op_派发；网络调用不进入持久方法。

队列方法与SettlementWorker一致，换前缀为compliance：claim/renew/release/prepare_dispatch/record_result/defer/reject_before_dispatch/accept_event。job额外request_template；归档prepare前事务外preflight，事务内复核固定输入再dispatching。查单永不POST。result scope沿F2 RESULT_SCOPE，outcome按action严格验证。

固定归档输入request.payload.archive_snapshot：target、content_version/compliance_version、event_cutoff、source_snapshot_hash、simulation、profile、retention、parent_manifest_hash、snapshots、required_roles、artifacts。artifact有id/path/kind/sha256/media_type/size/source；source三选一object_key、content_base64或provider_request+evidence+provider_kind。provider_request必须来自实际持久请求，禁用任意URL。ArchivePipeline.preflight(request)保存固定加密bundle；submit(request)只发送该bundle；query(request)只读；成功路径均逐对象版本读回，Pipeline不访问PG。

归档outcome：kind=archive、package_id、manifest_hash、manifest_member_id、seal_member_id、standard_profile、retention_class、retained_until、legal_hold、members（id/path/version_id/sha256/size/lock_mode/retained_until/legal_hold）、four_qualities、parent_manifest_hash、source_snapshot_hash、readback_verified。readback_verified仅客户端核实后生成。manifest不含自身hash，seal含manifesthash/内容版本/检测/锁证据但不自含未来seal锁，最终descriptor记录seal锁。全部正式标准profile未就绪，仅synthetic隔离profile可运行。
